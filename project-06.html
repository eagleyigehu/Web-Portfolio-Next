The Concept

Inspired by moments spent in and around the ocean, this design celebrates connection, movement and quiet wonder — bringing a sense of belonging to those who dive, explore, and protect.

The Inspiration

The design language grew from a personal experience learning to dive. Underwater, the only language is breath — one inhale and you rise. These gestures became the visual grammar of the work.

The Values

Respect the Ocean · Build Community · Stay Curious · Act Responsibly · Dive Deeper.

The Inspiration Behind the Design

Mom, I Have Something to Say

The product series draws its visual language from this personal graphic narrative — a story written while learning to dive in Lanzarote. The cobalt blue, the organic forms, the sense of weightlessness: all born from the moment of first neutral buoyancy, sixty feet below the surface.

About the Work

Mom, I Have Something to Say began as a letter and became a comic. The work documents three things the artist finally said — to a group, to the ocean, to a stranger on a bus — and traces a quiet return to communication.

Drawn during a trip to Lanzarote — a volcanic island surrounded by sea — where the artist obtained her Open Water scuba diving certification. The cobalt blue of the illustrations is the blue of that specific sea.
